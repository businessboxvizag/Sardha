Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains what personal data Saardha (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our app and website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow the principles of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Template notice: this is a starting-point policy for the Saardha team. Please have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer and update the contact details before relying on it in production.

1. Data we collect

  • Account & identity: name, mobile number, email, date of birth and gender (optional), and login credentials (passwords stored encrypted).
  • Delivery details: saved addresses and their labels (Home, Work), and, with your permission, device location to deliver your order.
  • Orders & payments: order history, item details, amounts and payment status. Card/UPI payments are handled by Razorpay; we store only masked, tokenised payment metadata — never your full card number.
  • Usage & app activity: searches, stores and items viewed, and cart activity, to improve the app and recommend relevant stores.
  • Device & log data: basic technical info (browser/OS), approximate location from IP, and sign-in records for security.
  • Support: messages to our in-app assistant/support, and your ratings and reviews.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and verify email and phone.
  • To take, deliver and track orders, and process payments and refunds.
  • To provide support and improve our stores, app and service.
  • To send service updates and, where you agree, occasional offers.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what’s needed to complete your order:

  • Delivery partners (Saradhis): your first name, phone and delivery address.
  • Store partners: your order items and notes.
  • Service providers: payment gateway and hosting/analytics providers, under contract.

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

4. Your rights

You may access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. Contact us below. You can edit most details anytime in the app’s Profile screen.

5. Retention & security

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, protected with reasonable safeguards.

6. Contact

For any privacy request, contact the Saardha Grievance Officer at privacy@saardha.com.
