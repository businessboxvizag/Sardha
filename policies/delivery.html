Delivery Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Saardha is a premium local delivery service: you order from stores you choose, and a Saardha delivery partner (a “Saradhi”) collects and delivers your order.

Template notice: starting-point policy — please have it reviewed and adjust fees, areas and timings to your operations.

1. Delivery areas

We deliver within our current operational zones. If your delivery location falls outside a serviceable zone, we may be unable to accept the order. Setting an accurate delivery pin or Google Maps link helps us reach you.

2. Delivery fees & charges

  • A delivery fee is added at checkout and shown before you pay.
  • Applicable taxes (GST) are shown separately on the bill.
  • Any store discount or promo code is applied before tax.

3. Timing

  • Estimated times depend on store preparation time and distance; they are estimates, not guarantees.
  • You can track your Saradhi live once the order is on the way.

4. Delivery & handover

  • Share your 4-digit delivery OTP with the Saradhi at the door to confirm handover.
  • Please be reachable on the phone number provided so the Saradhi can coordinate.
  • For pharmacy orders, a valid prescription and the required consent must be provided at checkout.

5. Failed or undeliverable orders

If we cannot reach you or complete delivery after reasonable attempts (wrong address, no response, or an unsafe location), the order may be returned. Refund eligibility follows our Cancellation & Refund policy. Perishable items may not be refundable in a failed-delivery scenario caused by incorrect details.
