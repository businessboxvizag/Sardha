Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Saardha account and the personal data associated with it.

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Saardha app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

Your account and personal details are removed immediately and you're signed out.

Option 2 — request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address (or with the phone number) on your account and ask us to delete it:

  • Email: support@saardha.app
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +91 86886 69816

We verify it's your account and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your personal data is permanently removed: your name, phone number, email login, saved delivery addresses, profile photo, saved stores, and support messages.

What we keep, and for how long

For legal, accounting and tax reasons, we retain order/transaction records (items, amounts, dates) required for the merchants' and our business obligations — but these are anonymised so they're no longer linked to you or your contact details. Retention follows applicable tax law (typically up to 8 years).
