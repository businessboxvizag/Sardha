Delivery Partner Agreement

Version 2026-08-DP-1 · Last updated 8 August 2026

This Agreement is entered into between Saardha (“the Company”) and the individual applying to work as a delivery partner (“Saradhi”, “you”). By e-signing in the app — typing your full legal name and confirming acceptance — you agree to be legally bound by these terms. Your acceptance, along with the date, time and device/IP, is recorded as evidence of a valid electronic contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Template notice: this is a starting-point agreement for the Saardha team. Have it reviewed and finalised by a qualified lawyer, and confirm it meets current labour, KYC and data-protection requirements, before relying on it.

1. Identity & document verification (KYC)

You must provide genuine, current documents for verification, both online (via an authorised verification provider / DigiLocker where available) and by physical / offline check by the Company:

  • Valid Driving Licence for the vehicle you operate.
  • Aadhaar for identity, and your current residential address and phone.
  • A clear photo of your vehicle showing the number plate, and the vehicle registration (RC) number.
  • A nominee / guarantor — one family member — with their name, relationship, phone, address and Aadhaar/ID.

You confirm every document and detail is true and belongs to you (or, for the nominee, to a genuine family member who consents to act as guarantor). Submitting forged, altered, borrowed or false documents is fraud and may attract action under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS) — including provisions on cheating and forgery (corresponding to former IPC sections 415–420 and 463–471) — and immediate, permanent removal.

2. Cash handling & settlement (money belongs to Saardha)

All cash you collect on delivery (COD) is Company money held by you in trust. It must be deposited/settled in full and on time. Retaining, using, or failing to deposit collected cash is a criminal breach of trust / misappropriation and may attract action under the BNS, 2023 (corresponding to former IPC sections 405–409), in addition to recovery of the amount from you and, where applicable, your guarantor.

A cash-in-hand limit applies; exceeding it or leaving it unsettled beyond the permitted window auto-suspends your duty until settled, and repeated default may lead to termination and legal recovery.

3. Vehicle, safety & conduct

  • You will hold a valid licence, registration, insurance and PUC, and obey the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 and all traffic laws. You are solely responsible for traffic offences and accidents caused by you.
  • You will handle every order honestly and with care, verify the customer’s delivery OTP, and never tamper with, open or consume order contents.
  • Abuse, theft, threats, intoxication on duty, or misconduct toward customers, stores or the public is prohibited and may be reported to the authorities.

4. Data & privacy

Your documents and personal data are collected and processed for verification, safety and legal compliance in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and our Privacy Policy. Aadhaar numbers are not stored in full; only a masked reference and the verification result are retained. While on duty you consent to sharing your live location for order tracking.

5. Liability, indemnity & recovery

You are an independent partner responsible for your acts and omissions. You agree to indemnify the Company against losses arising from your fraud, misappropriation of cash or goods, false documents, or breach of law. The Company may set off any dues (including unsettled cash) against your earnings and pursue lawful recovery from you and your guarantor.

6. Suspension & termination

The Company may suspend or terminate your engagement for unsettled cash, fraud, false documents, unsafe or abusive conduct, or breach of this Agreement, without prejudice to its right to report criminal conduct and recover losses.

7. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and disputes are subject to the courts having jurisdiction at the Company’s registered place of business.

8. Electronic acceptance

By typing your full legal name and tapping “I agree” in the Saardha rider app, you confirm you have read, understood and accepted this Agreement, and that your electronic signature is as binding as a handwritten one.
