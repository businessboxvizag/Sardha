Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This policy explains when a Saardha order can be cancelled and how refunds are handled. By placing an order you agree to the terms below.

Template notice: this is a starting-point policy for the Saardha team. Please have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer before relying on it in production.

1. Cancelling an order

  • By you, in the app: you can cancel an order yourself as long as it is still Placed — that is, before the store accepts and begins preparing it. Open the order and tap Cancel order.
  • After the store accepts: once preparation has started the order can no longer be self-cancelled in the app. Contact support and we will help where possible, but prepared or perishable items may not be refundable.
  • By the store or Saardha: a store may decline an order (for example, an item is out of stock or the store is closing). If we cannot deliver your order, it is cancelled and fully refunded.

2. Refunds

  • Online (UPI / card) payments: when an eligible order is cancelled, the refund is initiated automatically to your original payment method. Banks typically credit it within 5–7 business days.
  • Cash on delivery: no money changes hands until delivery, so a cancelled COD order needs no refund.
  • Delivery fee: the delivery fee is refunded in full whenever the whole order is cancelled before pickup.
  • If an automatic refund cannot be completed, the order is marked refund pending and our team processes it manually.

3. Partial issues (missing or damaged items)

If something is missing, wrong, or damaged on delivery, raise a support ticket from the order within 24 hours. After reviewing, we may issue a partial refund or credit for the affected items.

4. How to reach us

Use Help & Support in the app to raise a ticket, call, or message us on WhatsApp. See our Support policy for hours and response times.
