Delivery Partner (Saradhi) Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This policy sets out the terms for Saardha delivery partners (“Saradhis”). By onboarding and accepting deliveries, you agree to it.

Template notice: starting-point policy for the Saardha team. Please have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer, and confirm it meets local labour, KYC and data-protection requirements before use.

1. Eligibility & onboarding documents (KYC)

Before you can take deliveries, you must submit the following in the app. Uploads must be clear and valid; false documents lead to permanent removal.

  • Driving licence — a valid licence for the vehicle you use, with its number.
  • Aadhaar card — for identity verification.
  • Family member (guarantor): the name, relationship, and a government ID of one family member who acts as your guarantor. This person vouches for you and may be contacted regarding unsettled cash or disputes.

Your account stays in pending / under review until Saardha verifies these documents.

2. Cash handling & mandatory settlement

All cash you collect on delivery (COD) belongs to Saardha, not to you. It must be settled in full and on time. Holding, spending, or failing to deposit collected cash is a serious breach and may be treated as misappropriation.
  • Your cash-in-hand balance is tracked in the app as you collect COD payments.
  • A cash limit applies. When your balance reaches the limit you must settle before collecting more.
  • Settle your balance in the app (UPI). If your balance stays over the limit for more than 24 hours, your account is auto-suspended from going online until you settle.
  • Repeated or deliberate failure to settle can lead to termination and recovery action, including recourse to your guarantor.

3. Conduct & service standards

  • Deliver promptly, handle items with care, and be courteous to customers and stores.
  • Verify the customer's delivery OTP at handover.
  • Follow all traffic laws and use required safety gear. You are responsible for your own vehicle, licence, and insurance.
  • Do not tamper with, open, or consume order contents. Pharmacy and age-restricted items must be handled per law.

4. Payments to you

Delivery earnings and any incentives are paid per the payout terms communicated to you. Earnings may be adjusted for unsettled cash, penalties, or verified customer refunds attributable to your conduct.

5. Location & data

While on duty you consent to sharing your live location so customers and Saardha can track active deliveries. Your documents and data are handled per our Privacy Policy.

6. Suspension & termination

  • Saardha may suspend or remove partners for unsettled cash, fraud, unsafe conduct, abusive behaviour, or falsified documents.
  • You may stop working with Saardha after settling any outstanding cash and dues.

7. Acknowledgement

By ticking the acknowledgement in the app and submitting your documents, you confirm you have read and accept this policy, including the cash-settlement obligations.
