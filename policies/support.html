Support & Contact

Last updated: 8 August 2026

We want every Saardha order to go smoothly. If something isn't right, here's how to reach us and what to expect.

1. Ways to reach us

  • In-app tickets: open Help & Support in the app and tap Raise a ticket. You can attach a specific order. Replies appear in the same thread.
  • Call: tap Call us on the Help screen during support hours.
  • WhatsApp / email: also available from the Help screen for non-urgent queries.

2. Support hours & response times

  • Support hours are shown on the Help screen (configurable by our team).
  • We aim to respond to in-app tickets within a few hours during support hours, and to resolve most issues within 48 hours.
  • Live order problems (e.g. a delivery in progress) are prioritised — call us for the fastest help.

3. What we can help with

  • Order issues: delays, wrong or missing items, cancellations and refunds (see our Cancellation & Refund policy).
  • Payment questions and refund status.
  • Account, address, and delivery-location help (see our Delivery policy).

4. Escalation

If a ticket isn't resolved to your satisfaction, reply asking for escalation and a senior team member will review it.
